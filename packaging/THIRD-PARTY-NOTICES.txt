Third-party software in this build of Composa

Composa itself is MIT licensed; see LICENSE.

The Windows and macOS builds of Composa include Magick.NET, which is how they open HEIC, AVIF, TIFF and camera RAW files without ImageMagick being installed separately. Linux builds do not include it and use the ImageMagick that the distribution provides instead.

Magick.NET is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. It carries ImageMagick (ImageMagick License) and the libraries ImageMagick uses to read and write image formats, all compiled into the single native library Magick.Native-Q16-<architecture>.dll (or .dylib on macOS) that sits next to the Composa executable. Every component, its version and its full licence text is listed in ImageMagick-NOTICE.txt, which is Magick.NET's own notice file, shipped unchanged.

Several of those components are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, among them libraw (camera RAW), libheif and libde265 (HEIC). Composa does not modify them. The native library is loaded at runtime as a separate file, so it can be replaced with one built from source. The sources and the build scripts that produce it are published at:

  https://github.com/dlemstra/Magick.Native
  https://github.com/dlemstra/Magick.NET

and each component's own upstream source is linked from ImageMagick-NOTICE.txt.

Film looks

Every build of Composa bundles four film looks for the Color Lookup adjustment, derived from sguyader/FilmSim (https://github.com/sguyader/FilmSim) at commit 1453b2b55c48d99a889b1e455f91f6898ba2db41, authored by Sebastien Guyader and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 asks for nothing; this record is a courtesy and lets anyone re-derive and verify the files. The sources are 144-point HaldCLUT TIFF images, each resampled to a 33-point .cube by scripts/make-looks.sh:

  Fine Mono       from Acros.tif            SHA-256 d4c9f720c1588531b7f748e13505e11f392af663415e625bda0ff3455b41c7a5
  Muted Chrome    from Classic_Chrome.tif   SHA-256 cc303723b76205aedf970dc814dbecd6d9795955411aa2d6b265873864de7a66
  Standard Slide  from Provia_Std.tif       SHA-256 acf26aee152dbce9fff685aa727a5550855d425aa90610dd63b3ff2f15be0fe7
  Vivid Slide     from Velvia_Vivid_v2.tif  SHA-256 1b347b4c5afec78ad7f79103bf04b319e182b7133ffe2a8864c732a7003b7eea

The source files are named after Fujifilm film stocks. Those names are trademarks of Fujifilm, which the CC0 dedication does not touch, so Composa ships the looks under descriptive names and shows the stock names nowhere but here and in each file's provenance comment, as a record of origin.
